Supplements: Do You Need Them?

By Gillian Kagwira • 6 min read

Walk into any pharmacy and you will see rows of colorful bottles promising better health. But before you buy that expensive multivitamin, let's look at the science.

Food First, Supplements Second

The word "supplement" means "in addition to." They are not meant to replace a bad diet. Your body absorbs nutrients from an orange much better than from a Vitamin C pill because of the synergy of enzymes found in whole food.

The 3 Worth Considering

While most people can get what they need from food, there are three common deficiencies where supplements help:

  1. Vitamin D: Especially if you work indoors or live in low-sunlight areas.
  2. Magnesium: Our soil is depleted, making it hard to get enough from crops alone.
  3. Omega-3: Essential if you do not eat fatty fish (like salmon) twice a week.
