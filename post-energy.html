Top 5 Superfoods for Energy

By Gillian Kagwira • 5 min read

Feeling tired? You are not alone. In our busy lives, energy crashes are common, but the solution might be on your plate, not in a coffee cup. Here are 5 natural foods can boost your energy levels instantly without the crash.


1. Spinach

Popeye was onto something! Spinach is rich in iron, a key component in energy production. Iron helps red blood cells carry oxygen efficiently to your cells, fighting off fatigue.


2. Sweet Potatoes

Unlike white bread which spikes your sugar and then crashes it, sweet potatoes are a complex carbohydrate. This means they release energy slowly throughout the day.


3. Bananas

Ideally suited for a pre-workout snack, bananas contain potassium and vitamin B6, which help maintain blood sugar levels and muscle function.

Start adding just one of these to your lunch today and notice the difference!
