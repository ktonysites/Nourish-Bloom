Why Green Smoothies Work

By Gillian Kagwira • 4 min read

Green smoothies are not just a trendy Instagram accessory—they are a powerful tool for cellular health. But why blend instead of just eating a salad?

1. Breaking Down Cell Walls

Blending greens like kale and spinach breaks down the tough plant cell walls. This "pre-digestion" allows your body to absorb nutrients like chlorophyll and magnesium much faster than chewing alone.

2. Hydration + Fiber

Unlike juicing, which removes the fiber, smoothies keep the pulp. This fiber acts like a broom for your digestive tract, helping to regulate blood sugar while keeping you hydrated.

Gillian's Go-To Recipe

  • 🥬 1 cup Spinach
  • 🍌 1/2 frozen Banana
  • 🍋 1/2 Lemon (squeezed)
